Questions

How do I get my files?

Right after payment you land on a private download page. We also email you a link to it that works for 30 days. Download buttons refresh each visit (5 downloads per file).

What formats?

PDF (US Letter, printable) plus Excel .xlsx calculators that also open in Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Numbers. The 3-acre worksheet also comes as CSV.

Are these construction drawings I can build from as-is?

No. They are planning plans: AI-assisted concepts with dimensions, quantities and sourced cost ranges to help you plan, budget and talk to builders. They are not stamped engineering or architectural drawings. Have a licensed professional review structures, foundations, ponds, wells, septic and electrical work, and check your local codes and permits.

Where do the costs come from?

Published sources (extension services, government data, retailers and cost guides), each listed with a link and the date we checked it. They are US ranges, not quotes; your local prices will differ.

Refunds?

If a plan isn't useful to you, email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with your Stripe receipt and we'll refund you in full.

Is checkout secure?

Payment is handled by Stripe. We never see your card number. Your bank statement and receipt may show "Yaro Security", the business that runs our checkout.
