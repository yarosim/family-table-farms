Privacy

When you sign up we collect your first name and email through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) and use them only to send the free planner and occasional Family Table Farms emails. We don't sell your data. To be removed, email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com.

Purchases & refunds

Plans are sold through Stripe; we never see your card number. Stripe shares your email and the item you bought with us so we can deliver your download link. Your statement may show "Yaro Security". Refunds: email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with your receipt for a full refund (the download link is then switched off).

The plans page remembers the campaign tags in the link you clicked (for example from a YouTube description) for your browser session only, so we can see which videos lead to sales. No cookies or third-party trackers.
